# Caldera Pro landing page

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a small-business project manager, any industry, at a Windows desk in daylight. Job: decide to download the 30-day trial. Proof on hand: seven real app captures (Glass skin) and seven working demos. Must keep: the Glass screenshots and the demos' behaviour. Would feel wrong: every-SaaS-page, dark and techy, plain and corporate. Build path: code-led (no image generation in this harness).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one job sheet, and Caldera is the stamp that keeps it current: every date, total and status is inked and re-stamped the moment the plan moves. Refuses the centred headline over a framed screenshot and feature rows.

OWN-WORLD: Bright cool form paper with printed blue rules and small blue field captions. Headlines and statuses are rubber-stamp impressions with uneven inking in three pad inks: violet for time and the plan, green for money and done, red for changes and attention. Reverse-printed bands flood whole sections in those inks. Alternate Gothic lettering (League Gothic), Franklin text. Screenshots are stapled attachments. No cards.

STORY: The visitor sees a plan re-stamp itself, believes one change reaches time, money and people at once, learns agents work the same sheet, and downloads the trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Letterhead with the download button; the title set full width in stamp lettering; under it four ruled schedule lines with Drywall live at 8 days, overprinted by three large stamps, FINISH, COST and CREW. Changing the days re-hits all three with a thump, the old value ghosted beneath and a red REVISED stamp beside it. Download sits under the title.

FORM: Rubber stamps on a job sheet; position 4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: the thump. Motion grammar: every state change lands as a stamp impact; nothing else moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
