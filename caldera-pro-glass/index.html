Questions

Good to know.

Does Caldera order materials for me?

No. It finds listings, prices them and tracks each item from needed to received. You place the order.

Do I need an AI subscription?

Not for the schedule, the budget or your boards. Pricing, wantlist sweeps and the panel builder use the AI you already have: Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode, Ollama for local models, or a Claude or OpenAI API key.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or later, 64-bit. Run the installer and choose whether to install for just you or for everyone on the PC.

Where does my project data live?

On your own computer. There is no telemetry and no cloud sync. Caldera reaches the internet only when you use a feature that needs it, such as a pricing search, and installed copies check for updates.

Windows SmartScreen warned me. Is that expected?

Yes. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you on first run. Click More info › Run anyway.

Is there a free version?

Yes. The free edition is the original Caldera photo calendar, on GitHub.
