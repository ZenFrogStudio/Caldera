Features

A calendar that treats images as first-class data.

See a whole month at a glance — not as text, but as the moments that filled it.

Visual calendar grid

Each day shows a cover image. Hover a day to watch all its photos scroll past in a seamless loop.

Unlimited events per day

Give every event its own image, time and notes. Star one to make it the day's cover.

Paste, drop or pick

Add images with Ctrl+V, drag files onto any day, or browse with the file picker.

Analog clock scheduler

Click-drag arcs on a 12-hour ring to block out your day, with labels that curve along the arc.

Dark mode

One click in the title bar. Your choice is remembered between sessions.

Full-resolution lightbox

Open any image at full size for a closer look, right from the day view.

Clock scheduler

Plan your day on a clock face, not a list.

Switch from the grid to an analog clock tied to the selected day. Time blocks are arcs you can see — so you know how much of the day is spoken for.

  • Click-drag to create a block
  • Labels render as curved text along each arc
  • Colour-coded legend for quick edits and deletes
