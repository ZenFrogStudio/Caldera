## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and a little vanilla JavaScript in one file, no build step. This folder is the landing page for Caldera Pro; the app itself (Electron, Windows) lives in a separate private repository.

## Users

Project managers at small businesses, in any industry: often an owner-operator or the one person at the firm who "is the PM". They run a handful of jobs at once from a Windows PC, and today keep the schedule, the budget and the supplier search in separate places. (Confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-27.)

## Product Purpose

Caldera Pro is a local-first project workspace for Windows. It keeps a job's time, budget and resources on one plan, takes routine work off the PM's desk with AI agents, and shows where the job stands on dashboards the PM designs. Success on this page is a download of the free 30-day trial.

## Positioning

Stated by the owner as the selling points, in this order of emphasis:

1. **Synchronization between time, budget and resources.** Every Schedule line carries its dates, a fixed cost plus a daily rate, and an owner, and is paired with a task on the Manager board. Move one and the others follow.
2. **AI-native architecture for operational efficiency.** Every action in the app is one of 140 tools (24 read, 105 change, 11 run AI jobs, counted in v1.159.1). The PM, their panels, scheduled prompts, task agents and outside agents all work through that same layer.
3. **Materials found from a list.** Given a list of items, the app searches online stores and brings back a real listing with price, vendor and link, then tracks each item needed, priced, ordered, received.
4. **Options compared.** A wantlist hunt returns up to three listings, each checked against the stated must-haves; near misses are labelled.
5. **Changes and status tracked.** Tentative, costed lines waiting on decision gates; status on every line; a risk register and lookahead.
6. **A dashboard workspace the PM builds.** Boards of panels: charts, 3D charts, graphs and lists that redraw when project data changes.

## Operating Context

A desk in a small office in daylight, a Windows PC, the phone going. The PM's existing rituals are a schedule in one spreadsheet, a budget in another, supplier tabs in a browser, and status kept in their head or on a wall.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Windows 10 or later, 64-bit. Local-first: no telemetry, no cloud sync; data stays on the PC.
- Pricing: free for 30 days per computer, then $16 a month or $149 a year; up to 5 machines per account.
- AI features run on the user's own Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Ollama or an API key. They are optional.
- Wording that must stay narrow, because the app does not do more (v1.159.1):
  - It finds and prices online listings. It does not request supplier quotes and does not place orders.
  - There is no feature named "change order"; one is tracked as a tentative line behind a decision gate.
  - No 3D chart ships with the app; panels can draw one.
- No measured efficiency figures exist. Do not state hours or percentages saved.
- Undecided: the download URL (`downloads.zenfrogstudio.com/caldera/win/Caldera-Setup.exe`) is unverified; no public privacy or licence page exists to link to.

## Product Principles

1. One plan. Never show time, money or people as separate things.
2. Show it working. Prove a claim with the product doing the job.
3. The PM stays in charge. Agents work through the same tools, inside limits the PM sets.
4. Say only what the app does today.
